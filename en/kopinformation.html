Practical purchase info

Purchase information

Everything you need to know about how the purchase process works, reservations, pricing and what's included.

Step by step

Buying a kitten

We're happy to have a chat with you before you decide, so that together we can find a kitten whose personality suits you and your family.

Once you've found your kitten, it's reserved through a reservation agreement. The kitten moves at 14 weeks of age at the earliest, by which time it has been vet-checked, vaccinated, microchipped and registered with SVERAK with a pedigree.

Reserving a kitten

Reservation

A reservation means the kitten is set aside for you until delivery. We agree on this through a reservation agreement that clearly sets out the terms.

If you need to cancel your reservation, please get in touch with us as soon as possible so we can sort it out together based on what was agreed.

Pricing

Price

Companion cat

14 000 kr

Included in the price:

  • Neutering/spaying before delivery
  • Age-appropriate vaccinations
  • Microchipping
  • Veterinary health check
  • Pedigree
  • Hidden defect insurance
  • Kitten starter kit
  • Lifelong support

Breeding cat

Breeding cats are sold only to carefully selected breeders. Price and terms vary depending on the cat's quality, health, lines and breeding potential.

Pickup

Delivery

The kitten is picked up from us or from the relevant foster home once it's ready to move, at 14 weeks of age at the earliest. At delivery you'll receive the kitten's health record, pedigree and starter kit, along with all the information you need for a safe start together.

After the purchase

Guarantee and support

Our responsibility doesn't end with the purchase. You're always warmly welcome to reach out to us — whether it's about food, health, behaviour, or just to share a photo of how the kitten is doing. We're here throughout the cat's whole life.
