Bringing your kitten home

Everything you need for a safe and smooth start together with your new kitten.

A warm welcome

Welcome home

Soon it'll be time for your kitten to move in with you. It's a big moment — both for the kitten and for you as a family. Here we've gathered our best advice to make the transition as safe and smooth as possible.

Preparations

Before the kitten moves in

Here's what's good to have at home before the kitten arrives:

  • Litter box and litter (ideally the same kind the kitten is used to)
  • Food and water bowls
  • Food — the same kind the kitten is already eating
  • A cosy bed or blanket to sleep in
  • A scratching post or cat tree
  • A few toys to explore
  • A carrier for pickup and vet visits
  • A calm room where the kitten can settle in at its own pace
Food and feeding habits

Food

We recommend continuing with the same food the kitten had with us for the first while. The stomach is sensitive around the move, and switching food can easily cause an upset tummy.

If you'd like to change food later on, do it gradually over one to two weeks by slowly mixing in more of the new food with the old.

The litter box

Cat litter

Just like with the food, we recommend continuing with the same type of litter the kitten is used to for the first while at home. Recognising the smell and texture in the box makes it easier for the kitten to feel secure and keep using it as normal.

The first few hours

The first day

Let the kitten come out of the carrier at its own pace in the calm room you've prepared. Avoid pushing or picking the kitten up too much — let it explore on its own.

Show it where the food, water and litter box are, then give the kitten space to settle in. It's completely normal for the kitten to hide during the first few hours.

Bra att veta: Håll gärna resten av familjen, andra husdjur och höga ljud på lite avstånd de allra första timmarna.
Settling in

The first week

Give the kitten time to gradually explore more of the home at its own pace, rather than letting it loose in every room right away. Regular routines around food and play help the kitten feel secure.

Try sitting still in the room and letting the kitten come to you, rather than chasing after it. With time, patience and closeness, most kittens settle into their new home quickly.

Worth remembering

Insurance

Don't forget to take out insurance for your kitten as soon as possible after it comes home, in addition to the hidden defect insurance included in the purchase. Good insurance provides extra peace of mind if something unexpected happens.

After the move

We're always here

Our responsibility doesn't end when the kitten moves home. We offer support throughout the cat's whole life — feel free to reach out if you have questions about food, health, behaviour, or just want to share how things are going.
